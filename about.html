About CSC Project

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students access useful learning resources easily.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make learning resources simple and accessible to every student.

We provide useful resources for student, encourage effective learning, and promote easy access to information

Steps to Use the Student Resource Portal

  1. Open the Student Resource Portal.
  2. Choose a learning resource.
  3. Search for the notes you need.
  4. Open and read the study materials.
  5. Use the resources to support your studies.
